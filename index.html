How to play

  • 👆
    TAP a fruit to punch it.
  • ✊
    HOLD then let go for a POWER PUNCH — it smashes every fruit nearby! Hold longer for a bigger blast.
  • 🌳
    Fruit hide behind things and pop up to tease you. Punch them while they peek!
  • ⚡
    Punch fruit quickly in a row for a COMBO — up to x5 points!
  • ❤️
    Big fruit like pineapples and watermelons need more than one hit.
  • ⏱
    Punch enough fruit before the timer runs out to reach the next level.
